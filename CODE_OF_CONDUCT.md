# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## What we expect

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with the idea, not the person. Plenty of good designs lose to other
  good designs.
- **Welcome newcomers.** Help contributors understand the project and explain suggested changes.
- **Be clear.** "This breaks X because Y" helps; "this is wrong" doesn't.
- **Take the maintainers' time seriously.** Search before filing, and include what's needed to
  reproduce a problem.

## What isn't acceptable

Harassment of any kind; insults or personal attacks; discrimination or demeaning comments about
anyone's identity or background; sexual attention or imagery; publishing someone's private
information; and persistently derailing discussions after being asked to stop.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any other project space,
and to anyone taking part — maintainers included.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to **swap@usekopai.com**. Tell us what happened and where, and include links if you
have them. We will review the report, handle it confidentially, and explain our decision.

We may edit or remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when these expectations
are violated. We will explain the action where appropriate; serious or repeated violations may
result in a permanent ban.
